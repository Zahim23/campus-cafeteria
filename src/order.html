How to Order

Ordering at Campus Bites is quick and easy. Just follow these steps:

  1. Choose your items. Look through the Menu and Deals pages or the board at the counter.
  2. Pay at the counter. Tell the cashier your order and pay by cash or card.
  3. Collect your order token. You will receive a numbered token with your receipt.
  4. Wait for your number. Your token number is called or shown on the display when the food is ready.
  5. Pick up your order. Show your token at the pick-up window and enjoy your meal.

Peak Hours

  • Breakfast rush: 8:30 AM – 10:00 AM
  • Lunch rush: 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM

Order outside these times for shorter waits.

Pick-up Rules

  • Keep your token until you receive your food.
  • Orders not collected within 15 minutes may be discarded.
  • Check your order before leaving the counter.
  • Group orders: one person may collect with all tokens.

Tip: Have a complaint or suggestion about your order? Tell us on the Feedback page.
